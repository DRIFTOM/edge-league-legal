# Edge League Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 4 August 2026

**Privacy contact:** hello@edgeleagues.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of Edge League ("Edge League", "we", "us" or "our") collects and processes personal data in connection with the Edge League Telegram Mini App, Telegram bot and related technical infrastructure (collectively, the "App").

Edge League is an independent third-party application. It is not operated, maintained, endorsed or sponsored by Telegram.

## 1. What Edge League is

Edge League is a free-to-play social forecasting game created solely for entertainment and fun.

Users make predictions about future events using virtual points and may compete in leagues and leaderboards.

Virtual points:

- cannot be purchased;
- cannot be transferred between users;
- cannot be exchanged or redeemed;
- have no cash or monetary value; and
- cannot be withdrawn.

The App does not accept deposits, wagers or payments and does not provide cash winnings or withdrawals. It is not intended to be used as a real-money betting, gambling, investment or financial service.

## 2. Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies to personal data processed through:

- the Edge League Telegram Mini App;
- the Edge League Telegram bot;
- leagues, rankings and leaderboards;
- App notifications;
- customer support and user requests;
- administration, hosting, security and analytics systems connected with the App.

Telegram processes personal data independently in connection with the operation of the Telegram platform. Telegram's processing is governed by Telegram's own Privacy Policy and terms.

## 3. Personal data we process

### 3.1 Telegram account information

When you open or use the App, Telegram makes certain information connected with your Telegram account available to us. We read and store:

- your Telegram user ID;
- your Telegram username. If you have not set a username, your Telegram first name is used instead as your display name in the App. If neither is available, an automatically generated name is used;
- whether you have allowed the Edge League bot to send you messages.

The Mini App environment may technically expose further information, such as your interface language, colour theme or Telegram Premium status. We do not read, use or store this information, and we do not use it to evaluate, profile or rank users.

We do not receive your Telegram password.

We do not ask Telegram to provide your phone number, contact list, private messages or precise location. We will only receive additional information if you deliberately provide it to us through a feature that clearly requests it.

### 3.2 Information provided by you

We may process information that you provide directly, including:

- the country or region selected during onboarding;
- league names or other user-created content;
- notification preferences;
- messages, feedback or support requests sent to us;
- information included in a privacy or data deletion request.

The country selected during onboarding is self-declared. We do not use precise GPS data to determine your location.

### 3.3 Gameplay and league information

To operate the game, we process information about your use of Edge League, including:

- predictions you make;
- virtual points committed to predictions;
- prediction outcomes;
- scores, wins and losses;
- league participation;
- leaderboard positions;
- badges, streaks and achievements;
- invitations and referral codes used to join a league;
- timestamps connected with gameplay actions.

### 3.4 Technical information

We do not operate a third-party analytics or tracking service inside the App, and we do not place advertising or tracking cookies.

Technical information arises in two ways.

**Server logs.** Our hosting providers keep standard server logs for the operation and security of the service. These logs contain information such as the IP address the request came from, the time of the request, the address requested, and the browser or Telegram client identifier sent with it. We also record application error and diagnostic information when something fails.

**Operational records.** To keep the service running and fair, the App records a limited number of technical entries linked to your account, such as request-rate counters used to prevent abuse, and timestamps of administrative actions.

**Usage statistics.** We produce aggregated statistics about how the App is used. These are calculated from the gameplay information described in section 3.3 and are used in aggregate form.

We do not use this information to create advertising profiles or to track users across unrelated apps or services.

### 3.5 Public or league-visible information

Certain information may be visible to other Edge League users, particularly within public or private leagues.

This may include:

- your Telegram display name or username;
- your score;
- your leaderboard position;
- your prediction results;
- your badges, streaks or achievements;
- the leagues in which you participate.

Information visible on a public leaderboard may be accessible to other App users. Private-league information is intended to be visible only to members of that league, subject to the functionality and security limitations of the App.

## 4. Why we process personal data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

**Providing the App**

We use Telegram identifiers, gameplay information and league data to:

- create and maintain your Edge League profile;
- authenticate your App session;
- record predictions;
- calculate scores and results;
- operate leagues and leaderboards;
- provide badges, streaks and other game features;
- send service-related notifications requested or enabled by you.

The legal basis is the performance of the service requested by you and, where applicable, Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR.

**Security and abuse prevention**

We process technical, account and gameplay information to:

- detect technical errors;
- protect user accounts and App infrastructure;
- prevent spam, manipulation, duplicate accounts and abuse;
- investigate suspected violations of the App's terms;
- maintain the integrity of scoring and leaderboards.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure and fair service under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.

**Analytics and improvement**

We use limited usage and technical information to:

- understand how the App is used;
- identify usability problems;
- monitor performance;
- test new features;
- improve gameplay, onboarding and user experience;
- evaluate potential future improvements.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in developing and improving the App under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.

**Legal compliance**

We may process or preserve limited information when necessary to:

- comply with applicable laws;
- respond to lawful requests from authorities;
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
- enforce our legal rights.

The legal basis is compliance with a legal obligation under Article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR or our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR, depending on the circumstances.

## 5. Required and optional information

Your Telegram user ID and basic gameplay information are necessary to provide the App. Without this information, we cannot create your Edge League profile, record predictions, calculate scores or display league results.

You do not need to have a Telegram username. If you have not set one, your Telegram first name is used as your display name in the App instead.

Notification settings and information submitted through support are optional. Disabling optional notifications does not prevent you from using the main game features.

## 6. Automated test accounts

Edge League may use automated or developer-controlled accounts for:

- software testing;
- quality assurance;
- testing scoring and league functionality;
- identifying technical problems;
- evaluating possible future improvements.

Automated accounts are not used to artificially populate leaderboards, inflate user activity or create a misleading impression of the number of real users.

Where automated accounts are present in production data, we may identify them internally, exclude them from relevant statistics or otherwise prevent them from improperly affecting user rankings.

## 7. Automated scoring

Predictions, points and leaderboard positions are calculated automatically based on the rules of the game.

This automated processing is used only to operate the entertainment features of Edge League. It does not produce legal effects or similarly significant effects concerning users.

## 8. Notifications

The Edge League bot may send notifications relating to:

- prediction outcomes;
- league activity;
- leaderboard changes;
- available game actions;
- reminders connected with App features;
- important technical or policy updates.

You may be able to disable some or all optional notifications through the App or Telegram settings.

We may retain delivery records for a limited period to prevent duplicate messages, manage notification frequency and investigate delivery errors.

## 11. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

**Active account and gameplay data**

Basic account, score, league and gameplay information may be retained while you actively use the App, because this information is necessary to provide your profile, scoring history and league functionality.

If you stop using the App, identifiable account and gameplay data will normally be deleted or anonymized after no more than 12 months of inactivity, unless:

- you return to the App during that period;
- you request deletion earlier;
- retention is required by law;
- limited information is necessary to protect the App from abuse or defend legal claims.

**Analytics and technical logs**

Identifiable analytics events, technical logs and diagnostic information are retained for no longer than 12 months from collection.

After this period, the information is deleted or converted into aggregated or anonymized statistics that can no longer reasonably be linked to an individual user.

**Support and privacy requests**

Support messages and records connected with privacy requests may be retained for up to 12 months after the matter is resolved, unless longer retention is required to comply with legal obligations or defend legal claims.

**Deletion requests**

Where you request deletion, we will delete or anonymize personal data without undue delay, except for limited information that we are legally permitted or required to retain.

Deletion from our systems does not automatically delete information independently stored by Telegram. Telegram account and platform data must be managed through Telegram's own settings and procedures.
